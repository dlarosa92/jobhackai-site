Version: 2026-11-17. This is the agreed operating contract. Configuration is not activation or proof of a successful run. Historical instructions remain evidence, not authority to restart retired executors. Preserve original receipts, decisions, suppressions and exhausted sequences.

The first 30 days begin at actual activation. Target more than 100 distinct measured visitors on each surface and at least five genuine new Core signups, including free registrations. Exclude staff/tests where supported and disclose consent/bot/identity limits. A visitor on both surfaces counts in both; do not sum them as unique company visitors. Missing data is unknown, not zero. Fewer than 30 visitors on either surface or zero Core signups at day 30 calls for diagnosis and an owner-reviewed change recommendation, not automatic shutdown.

Local needs provider-specific website handoff evidence for three businesses: one EV, one junk-removal and one car-detailing business. Prepare a factual visual email/report for each. Separate listing exposure, outbound website clicks, contact actions and provider-confirmed outcomes. Clicks are not bookings, provider-site sessions or customer revenue.

Maximum total monthly business expenses are $500 for up to six months. Inventory subscriptions, variable costs and commitments before claiming compliance. No automatic new subscription, upgrade, top-up or purchase. Respect Marblism's shared 70-hour billing-cycle allowance and reserve service/correction capacity once. Start with 50/50 planned Core/Local effort; this is not forced alternation or an equal revenue target.

One shared D1 backlog and lesson record, one accountable owner, acceptance condition, next checkpoint and source identity per item. Product owns customer experience, catalog/release quality, tracking and financial accuracy. Marketing directs Sonny/Penny and owns campaigns and distribution. Sales directs Stan and owns fit, contact verification and individual conversations. Codex coordinates and independently verifies; Sebastian owns strategy, positive-reply review and commercial commitments.

Use GPT-6 Astra with high reasoning for Codex and substantive Product/Marketing/Sales owner work. Verify actual run metadata; global defaults alone are insufficient. Marblism uses its native runtime and is not claimed to use Astra. Customer product/voice models are separate and unchanged. Do not spawn all owners every two hours to repeat status.

## Cadence and capacity

One existing coordinator wakes every two hours around the clock, including weekends. It inspects due work and changed facts. It owns commissioning. Retired hourly, half-hour, separate-report and expired campaign executors stay inactive. Native tasks must not commission duplicate batches; preserve useful saved publication schedules and accepted assets.

One substantive daily huddle records verified work, today's priorities, constraints, actual disagreements and concrete handoffs. Later cycles handle changes, not simulated repeated discussion. Weekly planning and listing audits run within this same cadence. The provisional weekly baseline is six researched ideas adapted into nine social placements, up to five initial contacts per business line and one portfolio blog. These are capacity assumptions, not filler quotas or hard workload ceilings.

Social target: 21 days of independently accepted usable coverage, refill at 14 or fewer. Affordable fallback: 14 days, refill at seven or fewer. Blog cadence: weekly across the portfolio, every other week when constrained, with no newly fixed weekday. A topic or placeholder is not a published article. Use useful paid-for drafts first. Reduce extra adaptations and new sourcing before customer commitments, corrections and eligible follow-ups. Preserve templates, branding, signature/footer and accepted research-based imagery without people, including the supplied-for-intended-use exception. Video stays off. Marblism publishes social; Windsor reports and never becomes a second publisher.

Ordinary blockers escalate after two business hours of unsuccessful recovery, Monday-Friday 09:00-17:00 Eastern. Escalate sooner when a commitment is at risk; serious customer harm and uncontrolled spending require immediate escalation when detected. These clocks do not restrict research, social activity or owner alerts. Independent alert delivery requires its own verified transport; the local Mac cannot report its own outage while unavailable.

## Run receipts and recovery

Every pass reads this guide, the shared board and `/api/workflow`. The coordinator may run preparation and reconciliation stages and the once-daily coordination stage under the same executor. These are receipt types, not separate commissioning schedules. Server workflow modes and expected slots must match the actual saved scheduler before activation. Daily-slot policies give preparation/reconciliation one stable period per configured slot, including weekends and DST; daily coordination retains its Eastern date key. A policy update never starts an executor.

Start through `/api/workflow/start` and use its returned `runId` on writes. A reused running receipt belongs to its original execution; do not join it from a new invocation. A completed receipt means that period is done. One-hour leases and overlap guards prevent duplicate work. Never extend a lease or move a checkpoint to hide failure. Paused/retired workflows reject new starts but retain every unfinished attempt and overdue handoff.

`/api/workflow/finish` accepts only the same executor's unexpired running lease. It cannot close an expired attempt. `/api/workflow/recover` accepts an append-only acknowledgment from the original executor or owner after native side effects are inspected. Supply the exact expected actor, start and lease timestamps, resolution no_external_action or verified_side_effects, summary and HTTPS evidence. Unknown side effects stay held. Recovery cannot take over a live lease, edit the original run or create a successful execution receipt. Owner handoffs use `/api/workflow/handoff` claim/block/complete under an active preparation run, with expected version, evidence and a real checkpoint.

An advanced result requires saved changed work; checked requires fresh owned source/coordination evidence. Reconciliation requires fresh observations of Marblism, campaign, Gmail, directory and GitHub. A missing source cannot be claimed checked; finish blocked/failed with actual limits and continue independent work. Daily coordination requires the real owner assessments for its date. Incomplete copy/links/media are unfinished preparation, not external blockers. Finished owner-review packets do not stop independent work.

The independent server monitor runs every 15 minutes, records missed configured slots, expired attempts and overdue handoffs even when the Mac is absent. Ordinary notification eligibility begins after two Eastern business hours of the active alert episode. A recorded `serious_customer_harm`, `uncontrolled_spending` or `commitment_at_risk` escalation is immediate. Delivery still depends on enabled verified transport; dashboard state, provider acceptance, delivery and phone visibility differ. No notifications are activated by this guide or code.

Verify attachment/link support and actual owner delivery. Late data revises the same report with a labeled correction and preserved earlier evidence, not a duplicate initial report.

Report Core/Local/combined collected revenue, refunds, recurring versus one-time income, known costs, surplus, $5,000 milestone progress, Core signups/usage/paid retention, Local provider handoffs/feedback, completed work, failures/recovery, actual Marblism usage, lessons and next owner decisions.

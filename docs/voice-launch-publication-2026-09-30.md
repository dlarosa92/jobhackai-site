### 5. Follow-up exercise

This post has no image

Try this follow-up exercise before your next interview.

Pick one question and answer it out loud. Then ask yourself the question an interviewer would ask next: what did I personally decide, why did I choose that approach, and what happened next?

If that second answer is harder to give than the first, you have found the part of your story that needs work. Add the missing detail, then run the whole thing again. Keep it true to what you actually did.

More on building that answer: https://jobhackai.io/blog/how-to-answer-tell-me-about-yourself?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=voice_beta_2026_09&utm_content=voice_launch_followup_exercise_li_05

Preparing for an interview for a Product Manager role?

Choose Product Manager and the relevant seniority, then bring one example of a prioritization decision you actually made. Explain the trade-off: what you chose, what you set aside, and why. Be clear about your own contribution, then answer the follow-ups that ask for more detail.

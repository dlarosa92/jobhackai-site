# Directory category check — Northern Kentucky and Cincinnati

Checked September 20, 2026. Continue the mobile-detailing prototype as the first experiment. This is a judgment about a useful comparison product and manageable pilot scope, not proof of search demand, easy rankings or willingness to pay.

## What the public evidence supports

| Question | Mobile detailing | Junk removal |
|---|---|---|
| Is there something concrete to compare? | Package scope, vehicle size, extraction versus basic cleaning, and whether the customer supplies water/power. | Item types, pickup versus carrying from inside, minimum versus truck-volume pricing, address eligibility and scheduling. |
| Can customers already transact directly? | Yes. Pearl's publishes per-vehicle prices and online booking; Detling's has its own mobile service site. | Yes. Local GoodFellas accepts written inquiries; 1-800-GOT-JUNK offers online booking and an on-site estimate. A directory must add useful comparison beyond another company list. |
| What complicates a recommendation? | Two “interior details” can cover different work. A starting price is not a comparable final quote. Water/power access can determine whether a visit is feasible. | Public collection can be a better option for eligible items/addresses. Disposal exclusions and indoor carrying change the job. A paid-hauler-only list would omit useful alternatives. |
| Narrow content hypothesis to test | Help a driver choose an appropriate package and equipment setup, then measure a provider-website click. | Help a resident decide municipal pickup versus a paid cleanout, then route to the appropriate option. |

Pearl's explicitly says it brings water and rechargeable power, lists a maintenance full-detail package from $199, and distinguishes deeper-cleaning tiers. That makes equipment and scope useful filters, without claiming its service is better than another provider's. [Provider's mobile detailing page](https://www.pearlsautorecon.com/mobile-detailing).

Detling's serves Cincinnati/NKY and describes its own booking requirements and packages. Its source remains linked in the six-provider prototype dataset; no partnership or independent service-quality verification is implied. [Detling's](https://detlingsdetailing.com/).

For the alternative category, GoodFellas publishes a $125 minimum and volume-based pricing for larger jobs. Its contact form requires name/email and shows phone as optional. 1-800-GOT-JUNK serves the area, offers online booking, then gives an in-person estimate. These are provider-published offers, not comparable binding quotes or evidence of referral economics. [GoodFellas](https://goodfellasjunkremovalohio.com/junk-removal/), [1-800-GOT-JUNK Northern Kentucky](https://www.1800gotjunk.com/us_en/locations/junk-removal-cincinnati/us02030).

Cincinnati offers free scheduled bulk collection to eligible residential addresses served by the city, with item restrictions; private-hauler customers are not eligible for that city service. Covington describes weekly bulk-item service and a monthly resident transfer-station voucher, subject to its preparation and eligibility rules. Any junk-removal content should direct people to those current official rules before suggesting paid alternatives. [Cincinnati eligibility](https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/311/requests/mtl-frn/), [Covington guidance](https://www.covingtonky.gov/government/departments/public-works/solid-waste-recycling/trash-guidelines).

## Pilot decision and evidence still needed

Mobile detailing already has six source-backed candidate listings and a concrete equipment/scope comparison. Keep that small test rather than expanding the prototype to a second category before traffic and intake work. Competition exists in both categories; this research does not measure ranking difficulty or search volume.

After the held public launch is approved, test these specific questions with useful, sourced content:

- Which mobile detailers bring water and power in Cincinnati/NKY?
- What does an interior deep clean include compared with a maintenance detail?
- What should a customer disclose about pet hair, stains and vehicle size before requesting a quote?

Use a separate campaign namespace such as `directory_mobile_detailing_nky_2026_09`; never mix it with voice-beta campaigns or tag internal links with acquisition UTMs. Start with the comparison page and one practical guide. No ad spend, paid rankings or provider outreach is authorized by this research itself.

Report Search Console impressions/clicks when available, consented listing views/provider clicks, actual listing requests received in the support inbox, and any later provider-confirmed bookings separately. A click is not a lead; a request is not a paying customer. Subscription/placement revenue must come from collected invoices, not estimates of what a provider might earn.

A first evaluation can be scheduled four weeks after the actual public launch date. This is a proposed observation window, not a forecast. If traffic is too small, classify the result as insufficient evidence. If traffic arrives but provider clicks or received listing requests do not, inspect relevance and usability before purchasing traffic or expanding categories. Provider willingness to pay still requires a real written offer and acceptance after there is credible traffic to describe.

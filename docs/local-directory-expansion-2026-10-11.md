## Publication and contact rules

The three source JSON files generate category pages, provider pages, Markdown counterparts, agent discovery and sitemap entries. Generate and test the site, deploy it, then inspect the actual live category cards, provider pages, filters, source links and correction paths before notifying any newly listed business. Keep prior URLs and historical campaign attribution intact.

The October 10 Local catch-up already sent eleven verified introductions; never introduce those businesses again. Seven existing listings had no verified email and remain uncontacted. A provider's correction reply is a human response for suppression purposes whether or not its wording was AI-assisted. New notifications require a first-party published business email, a fresh Gmail/CRM/ledger duplicate and suppression check, and a live profile. Stan prepares category-specific copy; the existing business Gmail sender handles individually verified sends. The standing cap is five new first contacts per Eastern calendar day across Local and coach outreach combined. The existing one-follow-up rule applies only after a verified send and fresh no-reply, no-opt-out and no-bounce checks.

Report discovered, verified, published, notified, replied and held separately. A provider click is neither a booking nor revenue. Campaign links in external notification emails use the appropriate Local category namespace; internal directory navigation remains untagged.

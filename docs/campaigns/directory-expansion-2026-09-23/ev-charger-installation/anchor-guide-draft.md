# Comparing home EV charger installers in Cincinnati and Northern Kentucky

A useful quote starts with the home, the vehicle and where you want to charge. Tell the installer where the car parks, what charging equipment you are considering, and what you want the installation to include.

## Verify who will do the work

Ask for the legal business name and applicable contractor and electrician credentials. Check current status with the issuing authority and ask the local building department about registration for your address. A license number published on a provider website does not establish current status or authority for every jurisdiction.

Kentucky's Department of Housing, Buildings and Construction provides license search and verification. Cincinnati publishes contractor registration and residential project guidance. Requirements for other addresses should be checked with the relevant local authority.

## Ask for an assessment before assuming a panel upgrade

Have the installer assess service capacity, the panel, the cable route and your charging needs. Ask how the proposed design accounts for the home's existing electrical load. A spare breaker space alone does not establish capacity, and a panel replacement should not be assumed for every home.

## Separate hardware from installation

Confirm who supplies the charger, the intended model and connector, and who is responsible for equipment warranty support. If you are buying it yourself, have the installer confirm compatibility before purchase.

Request a written quote separating charger hardware, circuit and wiring work, mounting, any panel work, permit and inspection fees, testing, and any trenching or wall repair. Identify who applies for permits, arranges inspection and provides the completion record.

# Comparing junk removal in Cincinnati and Northern Kentucky

Start with the items and where they are now. A curbside sofa, an upstairs bedroom and a pile of renovation debris can require different services. Tell the provider what needs to go, how the crew can reach it, and whether there are stairs or parking limits.

## Check municipal pickup first

Cincinnati offers scheduled bulky item collection to eligible addresses receiving city trash service. The city's guidance describes service for buildings with one to four residential units; privately contracted collection is outside that program. Check accepted items, preparation and the scheduled date with 311 before moving anything outside.

Covington's guidance describes one bulk item per week for eligible residential collection and specific preparation rules for mattresses and upholstered furniture. It also identifies excluded materials and separate options for some larger loads. Use the current city guidance for your address. These are two local examples, not regionwide entitlements.

## Compare the actual job

Ask whether furniture, appliances, yard waste, electronics or construction debris are accepted. A business listing several categories may still exclude hazardous, regulated or unusually heavy materials. Ask about your specific items.

Confirm who does the loading. Some providers describe carrying items from inside the home; curbside service and a self loaded dumpster are different arrangements. Ask about stairs, disassembly and access before comparing estimates.

Find out how the quote is prepared and when it becomes binding. Photos can help explain the job, but do not assume an initial estimate covers every item or access condition. Request a written description of the work and any possible added charges.

## Use the directory as a starting point

The directory records what six providers publish, with sources and check dates. Unknown details are labeled. Listings are alphabetical, unpaid and do not establish service quality or a partnership. Contact the provider directly to confirm coverage, availability and the final scope.

Before you pay

Does the quote include getting it down the stairs?

Inside carrying, curbside pickup and a self-loaded dumpster are different jobs. Ask which one you are paying for.

## Check your existing options first.

Ask your building, municipality and regular hauler about bulky-item pickup, eligibility, placement rules and timing. Confirm what applies to your address before arranging removal.

[Check the municipal pickup guide and official sources](https://jobhackai.io/directory/junk-removal/#municipal-pickup).

## Five questions for each quote.

1.  **Do you serve my address?** Check building permission and the pickup deadline too.
2.  **Will you take these specific items?** Describe their condition and send photos directly to the provider if requested.
3.  **Who carries and loads?** Explain stairs, parking and access before agreeing to a price.
4.  **What is included, and what could change the price?** Ask about labor, transport, disposal and anything heavy or still connected to utilities.
5.  **What is the final quote for my job, and what is excluded?** Ask for the scope and price in writing before work begins.

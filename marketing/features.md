## From the first question to your feedback

Bring an example from your own work. Give yourself room to answer in your own words.

1.  ### Choose your role

    Set your target role and seniority. Add a job description if you have a particular opening in mind.

2.  ### Answer aloud

    Use your microphone to speak with the AI interviewer. Explain the situation, what you did, and what happened.

3.  ### Handle follow-ups

    Respond when the interviewer asks for more detail. Practice explaining your decisions beyond the first answer.

4.  ### Review your feedback

    Read what worked and what to improve. Choose one thing to work on in your next practice session.

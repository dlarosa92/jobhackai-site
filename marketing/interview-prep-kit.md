## One project. Three steps.

1.  01 / THE TEAM

    ### Name the result

    Start with what the team achieved, in one factual sentence. Choose work you can explain in detail.

2.  02 / YOUR PART

    ### Be specific

    Name what you personally did. If you supported the work rather than led it, say so. Name where your involvement stopped.

3.  03 / OUT LOUD

    ### Say it, then try again

    Use your notes as prompts. Let the wording change between attempts. Keep the facts consistent.
